PROBLEM STATEMENT 04 · DIGITAL FORENSICS

Examine media systematically, not by guesswork.

TruthLens analyzes images, video, audio and text, correlates the signals across sources and produces an explainable forensic report that a human investigator can review.

Start an analysis →

Workflow

Ingest

Upload media and related text. Originals are stored untouched with a SHA-256 hash.

Analyze

Each media type is examined with its own indicators.

Correlate

Signals are compared across files to strengthen or weaken findings.

Review

Inspect flagged regions, frames, segments and phrases.

Report

Export a traceable report with limitations and next checks.

Detection modules

VERTX-IMAGE

Noise residuals, error level analysis, edge and colour statistics, metadata, flagged regions.

VERTX-VIDEO

Sampled-frame temporal consistency, sharpness variation, container metadata.

VERTX-AUDIO

Dynamics, spectral steadiness, digital silence, high-band energy (WAV).

VERTX-TEXT

Rule-based phrase indicators with highlighted evidence in context.

Correlation engine

Agreement, conflicts, missing sources and shared generator tags.
